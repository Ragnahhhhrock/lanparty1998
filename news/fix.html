Bug fixes

Updates from the webmaster, newest first. NEW means we added something. FIXED means something was broken and is not now.

Show: Everything | New features | Bug fixes

7 Feb 1998FIXED The pizza guy knocks as you hang up
Order a large pepperoni and the knock on the basement door now comes the moment the receiver goes down, not half a minute later. Nobody knows how. Pizza Shack has stopped answering questions about it.
7 Feb 1998FIXED The pizza boxes are off the bags and the tower
Three boxes had been put down on top of things: one on the Cheez Curlz bags at the snack table, one jammed against the tower at PC-06 and the QUAKE box, and one on the open bag on the coffee table. Each has been moved to a clear patch of table or carpet, the HEXEN box has gone to the carpet to make room, the open Curlz bag now sits on the floor by the coffee table, and the second delivery goes on a free spot of table instead of on the first.
7 Feb 1998FIXED The mobile is off the couch
The Nokka 51 had been left on the middle couch cushion, which is a place nobody looks, and most of the party spent the evening ringing Dazza from the wall. It is now on the desk at PC-02, to the right of the mouse mat, where it can be seen from the middle of the room. Press E to pick it up. It rings the same numbers as before.
7 Feb 1998FIXED The wall phone is off the switchboard
The wall phone had been screwed to the wall directly in front of the main switchboard, so the breakers were behind the handset and the labels could not be read. It now hangs on the clear stretch of wall between the switchboard and the Volt Cola poster. The breakers are still not to be touched.
6 Feb 1998FIXED The pole by the taped man is gone
The white pole beside DUCTAPE has been taken out, and you can walk through where it stood. He is still taped to the duct. Nobody has explained that either.
6 Feb 1998FIXED The regulars stop talking out loud
Their lines are now text only, in the channel and the bubbles. The burps and snickers are still audible. N turns those off.
6 Feb 1998FIXED LANamp starts muted
The player now opens with the volume at zero so the room is quiet when you walk in. Drag the volume slider up to hear the playlist.
23 Jan 1998FIXED Eating arm
The regulars' arm stopped at the shoulder when they ate. The hand now reaches the mouth.
20 Jan 1998FIXED Chat on top of the meters
The chat feed no longer covers the meters, and the IRC nick list is wider.
17 Jan 1998FIXED Stale scripts
After an update your browser could keep running the old files and break the room. The page now checks for new ones on every visit.
15 Jan 1998FIXED The clock started at 11:42 PM
The party began at 11:42 PM, which is not when doors open. It starts at 7:30 PM now.
5 Jan 1998FIXED DOOM would not load on some servers
Where the game files should have been, the server was handing out a web page, and the emulator tried to boot it. It now checks what it was given and says "file not found".

12 entries. Found something broken? Tell the webmaster.
