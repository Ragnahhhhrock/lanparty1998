Welcome to LAN Party '98.

Six beige boxes, one 10BASE-T hub and a crate of Volt Cola. Sit down and frag.

This is the homepage for Saturday night in the basement. It is a real room: walk around it, sit at any free PC, and play DOOM, HERETIC, HEXEN, QUAKE, DESCENT and DUKE NUKEM 3D against whoever else is on the hub. Press Tab to get the mouse back, and click the room to walk again.

What's New

7 Feb 1998NEW Game cartridges, left where they were dropped
Somebody brought a bag of cartridges for the console under the TV and has not taken them home. There are grey ones on the TV stand and the shelf next to the console, one in the slot, and more on the coffee table, under it, and on the carpet in front of the screen. A few have found their way onto the PC tables. Six titles, all of them made up: Hover Rally, Snow Dash, Puck Smash, Dino Dig, Sky Pilot and Block Brawl. Mind where you step.
7 Feb 1998NEW This site has a front door
Everything in this browser can now be read outside the basement as well. What's New, Games, How to Play, the Guestbook, Links and Dazza's page each have an address of their own, such as lanparty1998.com/news, and they open in any browser without walking down the stairs. The news can be filtered the same way, at /news/new and /news/fix. The old .htm addresses still work.
7 Feb 1998NEW The CRTs show the game being played
Sit down at a PC and start DOOM, or any of the others, and everybody else in the basement now sees your game on the glass at your desk instead of the stand-in picture. Walk up behind someone and watch the run. With the lights off the tube lights the room in the colour of the level. It is a small, soft copy sent about five times a second, so it is good for watching and bad for reading the status bar. Stand up, or hit a blue screen, and the glass goes back to the stand-in after a couple of seconds. The picture is only sent while somebody else is in the room.
7 Feb 1998NEW The CD-ROM drives have a working eject button
Every PC in the basement has a CD-ROM drive in the front of the case, and the button on it works. Press E at the button to push the tray out or pull it back in. Three of the machines were left with the tray out and a disc sitting in it; those now close like the rest. The CD-R burner in PC-04 is the drive above, and it still only wants to burn.
7 Feb 1998NEW The pizza box has a lid, and it moves
Press F at a Pizza Shack box to flip the lid shut or prop it open again, or press E at a shut one to lift it before you eat. Some of the boxes in the basement were already open when you came down and some were shut, and the pizza guy brings his either way. The regulars lift the lid on a shut box before they take a slice.
