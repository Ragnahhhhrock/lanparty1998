Welcome to LAN Party '98.

Six beige boxes, one 10BASE-T hub and a crate of Volt Cola. Sit down and frag.

This is the homepage for Saturday night in the basement. It is a real room: walk around it, sit at any free PC, and play DOOM, HERETIC, HEXEN, QUAKE, DESCENT and DUKE NUKEM 3D against whoever else is on the hub. Press Tab to get the mouse back, and click the room to walk again.

What's New

7 Feb 1998FIXED The pizza no longer beats you back from the phone
The driver used to knock about half a minute after you put the receiver down, which meant Pizza Shack was cooking while you were still reading them the order. The oven now starts when you hang up, and he turns up about twenty-five minutes later, inside the thirty on the box. Long enough to lose a deathmatch and start another.
7 Feb 1998NEW The bean bag can be sat in
E at the bean bag drops you into the dent. There is a whump, a rustle of polystyrene and a squeak of vinyl on the way down, and you can look around the room from there. Any move key gets you out again, with the same noises in a lower voice. The dent turned out to be exactly your size.
7 Feb 1998NEW Spectator mode: K-Rad and n00bslayer play DOOM against each other all night
The two at the PCs against the right-hand wall have stopped fragging nothing in particular. K-Rad and n00bslayer are bots and they play DOOM deathmatches back to back, three minutes a match, on the real DOOM: it is the genuine game on both tubes, replaying matches recorded between two bots, with the status bar, the frag counter, the red screen and the body on the floor. Each CRT shows what its own player sees, so stand behind K-Rad and you watch his match, stand behind n00bslayer and you get the same fight from the other end. Four matches on three maps, then DOOM goes round again. Switch the lights off and both tubes light the wall in the colour of the fight. The two machines only run while you are within a few steps of the desks and not while you are playing one of your own, so a PC that is not being watched keeps the last picture it had.
7 Feb 1998NEW Voice chat, with a microphone
Press Y, let the browser use your microphone, and anyone else who has joined voice can hear you. They hear you from where you are standing, or sitting: loud beside you, fading as they walk away, and gone by the far wall. Y shuts the microphone and opens it again, and Shift and Y leave. Nobody is linked to anybody until they join, nothing is recorded, and a player in voice can see the network address of the others in it. A strict office firewall may stop a link from forming. Typing still works.
7 Feb 1998NEW Cans now open, and then get drunk
Taking a can of Volt Cola from the crate used to be silent until the hiss. It now has the tab lifting, the pop, the hiss, and then two swallows and a breath out once the can is up. Nobody has complained about the noise. Several people have complained about the swallowing.
