Welcome to LAN Party '98.

Six beige boxes, one 10BASE-T hub and a crate of Volt Cola. Sit down and frag.

This is the homepage for Saturday night in the basement. It is a real room: walk around it, sit at any free PC, and play DOOM, HERETIC, HEXEN, QUAKE, DESCENT and DUKE NUKEM 3D against whoever else is on the hub. Press Tab to get the mouse back, and click the room to walk again.

What's New

7 Feb 1998NEW The Amiga has nobody on it
xX_Ghoul_Xx has gone to PC-04 with his headphones and a copy of Descent, and the Amiga in the front-left corner is free for anyone. E at the desk and type at it. He will get up if you want PC-04. He says that is not the same thing as giving it to you.
7 Feb 1998NEW An Amiga 500, in place of a PC
The front-left corner no longer has a PC in it. xX_Ghoul_Xx has been given an Amiga 500, which he says is the only computer that has ever understood him, and it is running for real. E at the desk, he steps aside and you can type at it: the shell is open source and so is the emulator, so no ROM came down the stairs with it. Type HELP. Workbench is on the other disk. Dazza has it.
7 Feb 1998NEW The shop is now optional
Walk in goes straight down the basement stairs and you sit at any free club PC. If you would rather build your own, there is a button for that on the first screen: the shop, then the back table, same as before. Nobody is made to carry a tower down the stairs against their will.
7 Feb 1998FIXED The pizza guy knocks as you hang up
Order a large pepperoni and the knock on the basement door now comes the moment the receiver goes down, not half a minute later. Nobody knows how. Pizza Shack has stopped answering questions about it.
7 Feb 1998NEW The bean bag can be sat in
E at the bean bag drops you into the dent. There is a whump, a rustle of polystyrene and a squeak of vinyl on the way down, and you can look around the room from there. Any move key gets you out again, with the same noises in a lower voice. The dent turned out to be exactly your size.
